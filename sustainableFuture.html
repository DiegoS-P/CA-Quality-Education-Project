Quality Education

This site is about Goal 4 of the UN's Sustainable Development Goals, which is Quality Education. The goal has ten targets but I picked four of them to look at:

  • 4.7 Global Citizenship
  • 4.8 Safe Schools
  • 4.9 Scholarships
  • 4.A Qualified Teachers

Why does education matter?

Going to school is something most of us in Ireland don't really think about, but there are still millions of kids around the world who never get the chance. Without an education it's a lot harder to get a decent job or get out of poverty. The UN wants every child to have a fair go at learning by 2030.

4.7 Global Citizenship

Target 4.7: By 2030, make sure all learners get the knowledge and skills needed to promote sustainable development, human rights, gender equality, peace and global citizenship.

What is a global citizen?

Basically it means seeing yourself as part of the whole world and not just your own town or country. If something bad happens somewhere far away, a global citizen still cares about it and thinks about how their own choices affect other people.

What should schools teach?

The idea is that schools should cover things like:

  • Climate change
  • Recycling and saving energy
  • Human rights and equality
  • Other cultures and religions

In Ireland, subjects like CSPE and Geography already cover some of this, but in a lot of countries it isn't taught at all.

Why it matters

If people learn about this stuff while they're young, they're more likely to look after the planet and treat others fairly when they grow up.

4.8 Safe Schools

Target 4.8: Build and upgrade schools that are child, disability and gender sensitive and give safe, non-violent and inclusive learning environments for all.

What does a school need?

A lot of schools in poorer countries are missing basic things. Here are some of the main ones:

Facility Why
Clean water So students don't get sick
Separate toilets A lot of girls stop going to school without them
Ramps So students in wheelchairs can actually get in
Electricity For lights and computers

Feeling safe

It's not just about the building either. Students need to feel safe from bullying and violence too, otherwise they won't want to come in at all.

4.9 Scholarships

Target 4.9: Expand the number of scholarships available to developing countries for higher education, including vocational training, ICT, engineering and science.

What is a scholarship?

A scholarship is money you get to help pay for college. The good thing is you don't have to pay it back like a loan. For a lot of students in poorer countries it's the only way they could ever afford to go.

Who is it for?

The target is mainly aimed at students from:

  1. The least developed countries
  2. Small island countries
  3. African countries

How does it help?

When these students finish their degree, a lot of them go home and work as doctors, engineers or teachers. So it doesn't just help one person, it helps their whole country. Ireland actually runs a scholarship like this through Irish Aid.

4.A Qualified Teachers

Target 4.A: By 2030, increase the supply of qualified teachers, including through international cooperation for teacher training in developing countries.

The problem

In some countries there just aren't enough teachers. You can have one teacher trying to handle a class of 50 or 60 kids, which is about double what we'd have here. On top of that a lot of them never got proper training.

What could help

  • Training more teachers
  • Paying them better so they don't quit
  • Richer countries helping with teacher training

Why teachers matter

You can build a brand new school but it won't make much difference if there's no good teacher in the room. More teachers means smaller classes, and that means students actually get help when they need it.
