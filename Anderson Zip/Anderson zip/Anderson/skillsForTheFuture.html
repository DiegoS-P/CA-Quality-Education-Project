1. University Access

Higher education gives students the knowledge and skills needed to achieve their career goals. However, many students face barriers such as financial difficulties, limited opportunities and lack of information.

We should encourage equal access to universities and colleges by:

  • Providing more educational opportunities for students.
  • Offering guidance about university courses and career options.
  • Supporting students from disadvantaged backgrounds.
  • Promoting equal opportunities regardless of financial background.
