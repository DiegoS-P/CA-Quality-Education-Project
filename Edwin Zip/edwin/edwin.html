Barriers to Education

Education is an important part of building a better future. However, many people around the world still face difficulties when trying to access quality education.

Poverty, lack of educational facilities, disability and geographical location can prevent children and adults from receiving equal learning opportunities. These make it hard for individuals as well as society to grow and flourish.

Improving Literacy and Learning

Improving literacy is an important step towards reducing educational inequality. Reading, writing and communication skills help people access information and develop their knowledge.

Governments, schools and communities can work together to provide learning resources, trained teachers and accessible educational facilities. Technology can also help students access learning materials from different locations.
